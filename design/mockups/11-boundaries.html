Two sentences that are really one

The app breaks sentences by the standard rules, which cannot tell an abbreviation from a full stop. Where it guesses wrong, you fix it and it stays fixed.

Il saggio scende. | Nessuna riforma lo risolleva.

one sentence, and it should be two

What happens to the translation.
Joining puts the two translations together, in order, and the joined sentence goes back to unsettled — the text changed, so it needs another look. Breaking gives the first half the existing translation and leaves the second empty. Either way, Undo reverses it in one step.

The original wording is never edited here. Only where one sentence ends and the next begins moves — so the exported document still says exactly what the source said.
